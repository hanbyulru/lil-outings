Send as a Kit **Broadcast** to all subscribers on launch day (Friday after Thanksgiving), after the `holidays` branch is merged and deployed.

**Subject:** ✨ The Lil' Outings holiday guide is here
**Preview text:** Light displays, Santa visits, and tree lightings for little ones around Portland.

---

Hi there,

The holidays are officially here, and so is our holiday guide! ✨

We've rounded up the festive things to do with little ones around Portland this season, all in one place:

- **Light displays:** ZooLights, the Christmas Festival of Lights at The Grotto, Peacock Lane, and Winter Wonderland at PIR
- **Santa visits and storytimes** at libraries, farms, and community centers
- **Tree lightings, holiday parades, and menorah lightings** across the metro
- **Gingerbread houses and holiday crafts** for little hands
- **The Holiday Express** train with Santa aboard, plus tree farms with Santa on the weekends

On the site, pick **Holidays** in the "Something good" menu to see what's on today, this weekend, or next week, or open **Places → Holidays** for the seasonal spots.

[**See holiday happenings →**](https://liloutings.com/?utm_source=kit&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=holiday-launch)

Times and tickets change quickly this time of year, so check each event's page before you head out.

Happy holidays from our family to yours,
Han Byul at Lil' Outings
