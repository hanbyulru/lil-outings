# Lil' Outings: project notes for Claude Code

Kids' events, classes and places for the Portland, OR metro. Zero npm deps, Node 22, ESM, `node:test`. Commands are in README.md.

## Product decisions (from the owner, Han Byul)
- Audience: parents/caregivers of kids 0-12. **No adult-oriented content**: volunteer days, public meetings, adult classes, teen-only programs are excluded. Filter at the source where a feed labels them (`exclude_labels`), and the classifier is the backstop. Library scavenger hunts are hidden (owner, Nov 2026: `exclude_titles` on every library source).
- Content types: dated **events**, evergreen **programs** (classes without dates, e.g. PP&R), and **places** (directory).
- Landing page (Nov 2026): a daily check-in page. Headline "[Something good to do▾] with a [small person▾] [today▾] in [Portland▾]": all four underlined phrases are menus. What (`WHAT` in `web/app.js`, owner Nov 2026): All Happenings "Something good" / Storytime "Storytime" (… to go to) / Music & Art "Music & art things" / Science "Science things" / Animals & Nature "Animal & nature things" / Vehicles "Vehicle things" (was Trains & Planes, renamed by the owner Nov 2026; id stays `trains`) / Play "Playful things" / Other (no interest tag) "Other things"; only that phrase is underlined, "to do" / "to go to" follow outside the underline; underlines/carets are gray at their default, ink once changed (a remembered place counts as changed), and the what phrase takes its interest's color (Other: ink); it filters by interest tag and is not remembered. Who (`AGE_GROUPS` in `web/app.js`, owner Nov 2026): All (0–12, "small person") / Baby (0–12 months) / Toddler (1–2) / Preschooler (3–5) / School age (6–12, "school-age kid"); an event shows under a group when its age range overlaps. Age floors (`ageFloor` in `src/classify/rules.js`): chess is 3+ whatever the listing says (owner). When: Today / Tomorrow / This week / This weekend. Where: anywhere, a region, a district, or a town (see Location). Who and where are remembered in localStorage. The day's events are listed right under it, map on the right (on phones behind a Map toggle). From 6pm Portland time it opens on Tomorrow (`EVENING_HOUR` in `web/app.js`); "today" hides events that are already over. Events and Places are two tabs (owner, Nov 2026): a toggle centered in the header (no outline/background; on phones a floating pill fixed at the bottom of the screen) switches them (`#events` / `#places`, `showView` in `web/app.js`). The Places tab (owner, Nov 2026): same paper background, headline "Somewhere good to go in [Portland▾]" with the same location menu as Events (one shared choice; it filters places), kinds listed on the left by name only, the chosen kind's description under its heading and its cards on the right (stacked on phones); Public Playgrounds is hidden from the list (`HIDDEN_KINDS`). Age/day/what don't apply to places. The old search/filter card is gone.
- Filters (planned, partly removed from the UI for now): age band, 6 interests (owner, Nov 2026): Storytime `books` (yellow), Music & Art `art` (purple), Animals & Nature `nature` (green), Science `science` (blue), Vehicles `trains` (coral: trains, planes, cars, trucks, boats...), Play `play` (pink: playgrounds, stay & play, open gym, yoga, chess/games, family movies; not dinners or fundraisers, which stay Other; a storytime is Play only if its title says play). Old tags map via `interest_aliases` in vocab.json (music->art, animals->nature, dinosaurs dropped; `canonicalInterests`). Interests and categories are tags (an event can have several); cards show interest tags as chips and hide categories for now (owner, Nov 2026). `extraInterests` in `src/classify/rules.js` adds tags a title makes plain on top of Claude's (building/LEGO/maker = Science; dogs/cats/tails = Animals & Nature; reading/stories = Storytime); place names are not topics; a storytime or play program (playground, playgroup, playtime, open gym) gets Music & Art only if its title is about music/art (songs in the description don't count, owner); only Storytime and Animals & Nature read descriptions, other rule tags go by the title. Event color = first tag, category pills (All / Storytimes / Seasonal / Babies + Toddlers / Outdoors), free / drop-in / indoor, date, location.
- Location (owner's grouping, Nov 2026): 6 regions that feel like "a quick drive from home" (Downtown & Inner Core; SW Portland & Westside; South Metro; SE Portland & Clackamas; North, NE & East Metro; Vancouver, WA & Beyond) > districts > named towns/neighborhoods, all in `config/geo.json`. Picking a region must stay one tap; districts and towns are a "Narrow" step, where they are toggles: any mix can be picked (e.g. SW Portland + Beaverton & Cedar Mill, or Tigard + Metzger), stored as `{ picks: [...] }`, matched by any pick (owner, Nov 2026). A district can be in two regions (Tigard). Items get the district of the nearest named place within 5 miles (`nearestPlace` in `web/query.js`); farther out (Newberg, Sauvie Island) they show only under "anywhere". Vancouver comes from FVRL (BiblioCommons); far Gorge branches are excluded and anything 15+ miles from every named place is dropped as out of area.
- "Portland, OR metro" pill next to the logo (owner, Nov 2026): opens a dialog asking which city to launch next, then an email, then "We'll keep you posted!". Submissions POST to `SIGNUP_ENDPOINT` in `web/app.js`; empty = not sent anywhere.
- Look: Editorial direction. Palette Mosaic for big titles (h1/h2), Instrument Sans for everything else including the logo. Palette Mosaic's space is 1em wide: word gaps tightened (`word-spacing:-.4em`, owner), applied only once the font has loaded (`html.pm`); letter spacing -1px (owner). Rounded "mosaic tile" icons, not pixel-y. Site name is "Lil' Outings".

## State of the code
- Built in a sandbox with **no internet**: no adapter has been run against a live source. `npm run refresh:demo` exercises the real adapters on fixtures in `src/offline.js` (demo data, labeled as such in the UI).
- First live run (Nov 2026): `pdx_parks`, `pdx_parks_classes` and `wccls` work as written. `mcl` now scrapes multcolib.org/events-classes (`multcolib` adapter, verified). OSM Overpass is unverified (connection reset from the sandbox).
- Live runs from a proxied sandbox need `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1` (Node's fetch ignores HTTPS_PROXY otherwise). The offline demo caches under `data/cache/offline-http` so it never feeds the live run.
- Sources added Nov 2026: FVRL (Vancouver), Oregon City, Wilsonville, Lake Oswego, West Linn, Oak Lodge & Gladstone (see README table). Several give titles only, so tagging leans on titles; Claude handles that better than the rules fallback.
- More to explore (Nov 2026): a list of kinds, each with its own mosaic tile icon (`EXPLORE_LOOK` in `web/app.js`), from `config/vocab.json` `explore` (Indoor Playgrounds, Public Playgrounds, Music & Art, Science, Nature, Farms, History, Vehicles (id `trains_planes`), Gymnastics, Eateries: cafes and restaurants with play spaces, yellow, owner Nov 2026) over the places directory; Gymnastics also lists the PP&R class series that are gymnastics/tumbling, plus OSM `sport=gymnastics` gyms. Music classes go under Music & Art (owner, Nov 2026). Curated places (`config/places.seed.json`) only with an address confirmed by OSM/Nominatim or consistent sources; OSM places get kinds from their tags (`exploreFor`; untyped museums by name, customer-only playgrounds = indoor play). Most OSM playgrounds are unnamed: each named park (bounding box, `parkAt`) contributes one, called "<Park> playground", carrying the park's Wikidata/photo tags. The old "Browse by interest" section is gone (owner's call). Corrections to OSM places (dead links etc.) go in `config/place_overrides.json`, keyed by place id.
- Place photos (Nov 2026): card banners come from Wikimedia Commons (free licenses; credit links to the file page), resolved in `src/images.js` from a seed's `commons` file or `wikidata` QID (P18), or OSM `wikidata`/`wikimedia_commons`/`image` tags (a playground's Wikidata photo only if the item is a park). Owner's OK: places of every visible kind with no Commons photo use their own website's share image (og:image), credited "Photo: <site>" and linking to it (`addWebsiteImages`, `WEBSITE_PHOTO_KINDS`); for businesses (Indoor Playgrounds, Farms, Gymnastics, Music & Art, Eateries: `WEBSITE_LOGO_KINDS`), if the site has no photo, its logo (share image, JSON-LD logo, logo <img>, apple-touch-icon) is shown whole on a tinted tile (`image_logo`, owner's call: don't skip logos); museums and parks never get a logo (it's usually the city's). Public Playgrounds stay Commons-only. Sites that block bots (403/Cloudflare) are not worked around. No photo, or one that fails to load: a mosaic banner in the kind's color. Wikimedia rate limits generic User-Agents (`WIKIMEDIA_UA`).
- City Parks & Rec calendars added: Lake Oswego, West Linn, Milwaukie, Wilsonville, Sherwood, Happy Valley, THPRD, Tualatin, NCPRD, Gresham, Vancouver (README table). Not yet: Tigard and Hillsboro (403 to bots), Beaverton (new client-rendered CivicPlus site; THPRD covers Beaverton's parks).
- Geocoding (Nov 2026): sources with `config.geocode` get street addresses placed by Nominatim (`src/geocode.js`; cached six months, at most 60 new lookups a run), else the adapter's town-level `geo_fallback`. The offline demo uses the fallback only.
- Next jobs: Tigard/Hillsboro/Beaverton calendars; ActiveNet dated-session adapter for PP&R; review UI for `review.json`.
- Map: real basemap done (MapLibre + self-hosted Protomaps tiles, `web/map.js`); same-spot items share one pin with a list card.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages via `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (daily refresh + deploy). The site is static (`npm run build:site` -> `_site/`); `src/server.js` is for local dev.

## Conventions
- Adapters return raw events/places/programs; normalization, dedupe, tagging and geo happen in `src/pipeline.js`.
- LLM tagging output is validated against `config/vocab.json`; listings are untrusted input. Never let model output add vocabulary.
- Every parser gets a fixture test. Keep `web/query.js` free of Node APIs (shared by server and browser).
- Place/ZIP coordinates are hand-entered approximate centers (`config/geo.json`). The old NW/N/NE/SW/SE quadrants are gone.
