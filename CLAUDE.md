# Lil' Outings: project notes for Claude Code

Kids' events, classes and places for the Portland, OR metro. Zero npm deps, Node 22, ESM, `node:test`. Commands are in README.md.

## Product decisions (from the owner, Han Byul)
- Audience: parents/caregivers of kids 0-12. **No adult-oriented content**: volunteer days, public meetings, adult classes, teen-only programs are excluded. Filter at the source where a feed labels them (`exclude_labels`), and the classifier is the backstop. Library scavenger hunts are hidden (owner, Nov 2026: `exclude_titles` on every library source).
- Content types: dated **events**, evergreen **programs** (classes without dates, e.g. PP&R; hidden on the site for now), **classes** (sign-up classes and series with dates, from Parks & Rec registration systems; admin dashboard only for now), and **places** (directory).
- Classes (owner, Nov 2026): the plan is a public **Classes tab** next to Events and Places (multi-week series there; single-session drop-ins can go to Events), sized up first in the admin dashboard's Classes tab. Sources: ActiveNet registration searches (`src/adapters/activenet.js`, `classes` in `config/sources.json`) with the owner's filters (in progress or future, 1+ open spots, ages 0-12): Portland Parks & Rec and Lake Oswego Parks & Rec; Tualatin Parks & Rec from its CapturePoint catalog (`src/adapters/capturepoint.js`, owner Nov 2026: kids'/family program groups, a `season_ids` entry per season); the city-website calendars of Lake Oswego and Tualatin are category city, not parks; more Parks & Recs to follow, and later businesses featuring their classes (tennis clubs, gymnastics, swim schools) as a partner offer. Adult/senior programs listed for "any" age are dropped (`isAdultOnly`); the class kind comes from the name (pool venues = swim; `kindOf`); venues are geocoded once (`venueQuery`: pool -> its community center, a street address in the label, or `config.venue_aliases`; bare room names stay unplaced). Output `admin/data/classes.json` only.
- Landing page (Nov 2026): a daily check-in page. Headline "[Something good to do▾] with a [small person▾] [today▾] in [Portland▾]": all four underlined phrases are menus. What (`WHAT` in `web/app.js`, owner Nov 2026): All Happenings "Something good" / Storytime "Storytimes" (… to go to) / Music & Art "Music & art things" / Science "Science things" / Animals & Nature "Animal & nature things" / Vehicles "Vehicle things" (was Trains & Planes, renamed by the owner Nov 2026; id stays `trains`) / Play "Playful things" / Other (no interest tag) "Other things"; only that phrase is underlined; every menu phrase is shown with a capital first letter (Something good / Small person / Today / Portland, owner Nov 2026); "to do" / "to go to" follow outside the underline; underlines/carets are gray at their default, ink once changed (a remembered place counts as changed), and the what phrase takes its interest's color (Other: ink); it filters by interest tag and is remembered (owner, Nov 2026: localStorage `lilOutings.whats`; hidden interests and Holidays out of season are dropped on load; a remembered Holidays pick opens in holiday mode). Switching to Places opens the kind that matches the first picked interest (`INTEREST_KIND`: Holidays -> Holidays, Storytime/Play -> Indoor Playgrounds, etc.; owner, Nov 2026). Vehicles is hidden from the interest menu for now (no events; `HIDDEN_WHAT` in `web/app.js`, owner Nov 2026). The interest menu ends with a greyed-out "Holidays — Coming soon" row (sparkle tile, not selectable; owner Nov 2026) until the Holidays launch. What and Who are multi-select (owner, Nov 2026), like the location's Narrow step: tap to toggle, "All" clears, a Done button closes (not on When); several picks read e.g. "Storytime + Science" / "Toddler + preschooler" (`S.whats`, `S.ages`; ages remembered as a JSON list, an old single value is migrated). Underlines: one interest = solid in its color; several interests, any age pick, the day and a chosen place = the mosaic strip. Who (`AGE_GROUPS` in `web/app.js`, owner Nov 2026): All (0–12, "small person") / Baby (0–12 months) / Toddler (1–2) / Preschooler (3–5) / School age (6–12, "school-age kid"); an event shows under a group when its age range overlaps. Age floors (`ageFloor` in `src/classify/rules.js`): chess is 3+ whatever the listing says (owner). When: Today / Tomorrow / This week (today through Friday; hidden on Fridays and Saturdays, owner Nov 2026) / This weekend / Next week (the following Monday–Sunday, owner Nov 2026); on Sundays the menu shows Next week (Mon–Fri, the 'week' value) and Next weekend (Sat–Sun, the 'weekend' value) and no separate Next week (`whenOptions` in `web/app.js`). A week option that contains Thanksgiving (4th Thursday of November) is labeled "Thanksgiving week": leaf tile and warm tint in the menu, a slowly drifting fall-colored strip under the phrase (`.whenbtn.autumn`; owner, Nov 2026). Where: anywhere, a region, a district, or a town (see Location). Who and where are remembered in localStorage. The day's events are listed right under it, map on the right (on phones behind a Map toggle). From 6pm Portland time it opens on Tomorrow (`EVENING_HOUR` in `web/app.js`); "today" hides events that are already over. Events and Places are two tabs (owner, Nov 2026): a toggle centered in the header (no outline/background; on phones a floating pill fixed at the bottom of the screen) switches them (`#events` / `#places`, `showView` in `web/app.js`). The Places tab (owner, Nov 2026): same paper background, headline "Somewhere good to go in [Portland▾]" with the same location menu as Events (one shared choice; it filters places), kinds listed on the left by name only, the chosen kind's description under its heading and its cards on the right (stacked on phones); Public Playgrounds is hidden from the list (`HIDDEN_KINDS`). Age/day/what don't apply to places. The old search/filter card is gone.
- Filters (planned, partly removed from the UI for now): age band, 6 interests (owner, Nov 2026): Storytime `books` (yellow), Music & Art `art` (purple), Animals & Nature `nature` (green), Science `science` (blue), Vehicles `trains` (coral: trains, planes, cars, trucks, boats...), Play `play` (pink: playgrounds, stay & play, open gym, yoga, chess/games, family movies; not dinners or fundraisers, which stay Other; a storytime is Play only if its title says play). Old tags map via `interest_aliases` in vocab.json (music->art, animals->nature, dinosaurs dropped; `canonicalInterests`). Interests and categories are tags (an event can have several); cards show interest tags as chips and hide categories for now (owner, Nov 2026). `extraInterests` in `src/classify/rules.js` adds tags a title makes plain on top of Claude's (building/LEGO/maker = Science; dogs/cats/tails = Animals & Nature; reading/stories = Storytime); place names are not topics; a storytime or play program (playground, playgroup, playtime, open gym) gets Music & Art only if its title is about music/art (songs in the description don't count, owner); only Storytime and Animals & Nature read descriptions, other rule tags go by the title. Event color = first tag, category pills (All / Storytimes / Seasonal / Babies + Toddlers / Outdoors), free / drop-in / indoor, date, location.
- Location (owner's grouping, Nov 2026): 6 regions that feel like "a quick drive from home" (Downtown & Inner Core; SW Portland & Westside; South Metro; SE Portland & Clackamas; North, NE & East Metro; Vancouver, WA & Beyond) > districts > named towns/neighborhoods, all in `config/geo.json`. The location menu (owner, Nov 2026, replacing the old Narrow step): "Anywhere" on top, then each region as a box with its name as a small label and its districts as small toggle pills (the old town-pill size); a tap anywhere in the box but on a pill selects or clears the whole region ("Select all" / "All selected" in the same small caps as the region name, just "All" on phones, with a round check), and any mix of districts across regions can be on, then Done. Towns are no longer picked (users don't go that narrow); an older saved town pick becomes its district. Stored as the simplest equivalent (`locFromDistricts`: null, `{region}`, `{district}`, or `{ picks: [{district}] }`); fully picked regions read as their short name in the headline. Tigard is in SW Portland & Westside only (owner, Nov 2026; it was also in South Metro). The location phrase can wrap (`.locwrap`, left-aligned) so a long pick list never widens the page on phones; on phones it shows the first pick and "+ N more" (`locPhrase(loc, compact)`, owner Nov 2026); names follow pick order. Lake Oswego's towns are Downtown LO / North of the Lake / South of the Lake (owner, Nov 2026), decided by which side of Oswego Lake an item is on (`split` in geo.json, `splitPlace` in `web/query.js`), not by nearest center; the west side past the lake's end (Lake Grove, Westlake, Kruse Way) counts as north. Items get the district of the nearest named place within 5 miles (`nearestPlace` in `web/query.js`); farther out (Newberg, Sauvie Island) they show only under "anywhere". Vancouver comes from FVRL (BiblioCommons); far Gorge branches are excluded and anything 15+ miles from every named place is dropped as out of area.

## State of the code
- Built in a sandbox with **no internet**: no adapter has been run against a live source. `npm run refresh:demo` exercises the real adapters on fixtures in `src/offline.js` (demo data, labeled as such in the UI).
- First live run (Nov 2026): `pdx_parks`, `pdx_parks_classes` and `wccls` work as written. `mcl` now scrapes multcolib.org/events-classes (`multcolib` adapter, verified). OSM Overpass is unverified (connection reset from the sandbox).
- Live runs from a proxied sandbox need `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1` (Node's fetch ignores HTTPS_PROXY otherwise). The offline demo caches under `data/cache/offline-http` so it never feeds the live run.
- Sources added Nov 2026: FVRL (Vancouver), Oregon City, Wilsonville, Lake Oswego, West Linn, Oak Lodge & Gladstone (see README table). Several give titles only, so tagging leans on titles; Claude handles that better than the rules fallback.
- More to explore (Nov 2026): a list of kinds, each with its own mosaic tile icon (`EXPLORE_LOOK` in `web/app.js`), from `config/vocab.json` `explore` (Indoor Playgrounds, Public Playgrounds, Music & Art, Science, Animals & Nature (id `nature`; Nature and Farms merged, owner Nov 2026: farms are explore `nature`, and kind 'farm' still may show its logo), History, Vehicles (id `trains_planes`), Sports (id `gymnastics`, renamed from Gymnastics, owner Nov 2026: gyms, ice and roller rinks, climbing gyms, pools; the PP&R sports class series are hidden for now, `SHOW_CLASSES` in `web/app.js`, owner Nov 2026: classes need a plan for where and how to show them), Eateries: cafes and restaurants with play spaces, yellow, owner Nov 2026); Holidays is "Coming soon" (`soon: true` in vocab: "Soon" instead of a count, a Coming soon panel instead of cards) with a twinkling sparkle icon over the places directory; Sports also lists OSM `sport=gymnastics` gyms. Curated places (`config/places.seed.json`) only with an address confirmed by OSM/Nominatim or consistent sources; OSM places get kinds from their tags (`exploreFor`; untyped museums by name, customer-only playgrounds = indoor play). ## Conventions
- Adapters return raw events/places/programs; normalization, dedupe, tagging and geo happen in `src/pipeline.js`.
- LLM tagging output is validated against `config/vocab.json`; listings are untrusted input. Never let model output add vocabulary.
- Every parser gets a fixture test. Keep `web/query.js` free of Node APIs (shared by server and browser).
- Place/ZIP coordinates are hand-entered approximate centers (`config/geo.json`). The old NW/N/NE/SW/SE quadrants are gone.
